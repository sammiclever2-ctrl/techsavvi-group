TechSavvi Labs

Where we try new ideas before anyone else has to.

Labs is our research and development team. We build small prototypes to test new technology, find out what actually works, and bring the best of it into our client work and Academy courses.

Focus areas

What we're exploring

AI assistants & automation

Using language models to answer customer questions, process documents and handle routine tasks for small businesses.

Data & machine learning

Models and tools that help organisations understand their data, from credit and risk insights to forecasting.

Learning technology

Interactive tools, platforms and games that make learning more engaging, shaped by what we see in our own Academy classes.

How Labs works

From idea to something you can use

  1. Question

    Start with a real problem we, a client or a student has.

  2. Prototype

    Build the smallest version that can answer the question.

  3. Test

    Put it in front of real users and see what holds up.

  4. Share

    Turn what works into a client service or an Academy lesson.
