Questions

Frequently asked

How much does a project cost?+

It depends on what you need. After a short call, we send a written quote with a fixed price and timeline, so there are no surprises. We can also split larger projects into phases.

How long does it take?+

A company website is usually a matter of weeks. Larger apps take longer and are planned in stages. You'll get a timeline with your quote.

Do you work with international clients?+

Yes. We work with clients in different countries and time zones, fully remotely, using video calls, email, WhatsApp and shared project boards.

What happens after launch?+

We show your team how to use and update what we built. We can also stay on for hosting, fixes and new features, agreed as part of your quote.

Will I own the code and content?+

Yes. Once the project is paid for, the code, design and content are yours.
