About TechSavvi Group

A technology company built for a global market.

We started TechSavvi Group in 2024 with a simple belief: every business deserves well-built software, and anyone with the drive to learn deserves a real path into a tech career.

Our story

Why we exist

Many businesses and organisations still run on paper, spreadsheets and WhatsApp messages. They know software could help, but good developers are hard to find and often far away.

At the same time, many talented people want to work in tech but can't find practical, hands-on training that prepares them for real work.

TechSavvi Group brings both together. Solutions builds software for clients. Labs tests new technology so we know what's worth using. Academy teaches what we learn, so more people can do this work.

Our mission

Help organisations work better with technology, and help people build careers in it.

Clear communication

Plain language, written quotes and regular updates. No jargon, no surprises.

Quality work

We build with proven tools and test before we launch, so what we deliver keeps working.

Growing talent

We invest in the next generation of tech professionals through the Academy.
